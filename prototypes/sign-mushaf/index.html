Sign Mushaf: letters and Al-Fatihah

Every Arabic letter of the Uthmani text fingerspelled, with harakat shown as movement marks, following the method of LPMQ's Pedoman Membaca Mushaf Al-Qur'an Isyarat (Kemenag RI, 2022). Al-Fatihah below is converted by Bayaan's rule-based converter, not drawn by hand.

Illustrations: LPMQ Kemenag RI, used for internal review pending permission; not for distribution.

Method follows LPMQ's Pedoman Membaca Mushaf Al-Qur'an Isyarat (Kemenag RI). Prototype pending review by Deaf teachers and LPMQ.

Surah Al-Fatihah

Read each row right to left, as in the guideline. Kitabah signs what is written; Tilawah signs what is recited. Words underlined in orange sign differently from the guideline's worked example because Bayaan's Madani text is spelled differently from the Mushaf Standar Indonesia the example uses.

The alphabet

The 32 handshapes of the guideline's letter table, in its order, plus the Tilawah form of hamza. Drawn from the viewer's side: the signer's right hand, palm descriptions in the signer's frame.

Harakat and marks

Each mark is a movement made after forming the letter. The glyphs are the guideline's; arrows point the way the viewer sees the hand move.

Text: Bayaan's KFGQPC Hafs Uthmani text; transliteration from Bayaan's data. Method and illustrations: Lajnah Pentashihan Mushaf Al-Qur'an (LPMQ), Kemenag RI, used for internal review pending permission; not for distribution.
